Read [start-here](start-here) first: how to read this wiki and how to call tools.
`system__wiki_read` and `system__wiki_search` navigate the wiki by path or search; `system__call_tool` runs any tool by its scoped id.
The generated pages under [reference/tools](reference/tools) carry the exact, validating call for every tool; the live map under [this-install](this-install) names the agents, graphs, collections and providers of this install.
