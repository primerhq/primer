Read [start-here](start-here) first: how to read this wiki and how to call tools.
For a goal, the purpose-mapped index [purpose-map](purpose-map) maps "I want to do X" to the page that says how, and the runbooks under [runbooks](runbooks) dictate the flows end to end.
`system__wiki_read` and `system__wiki_search` navigate the wiki by path or search; `system__call_tool` runs any tool by its `toolset_id` and `tool_name`.
The generated pages under [reference/tools](reference/tools) carry the exact, validating call for every tool; the live map under [this-install](this-install) names the agents, graphs, collections and providers of this install.
